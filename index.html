FEATURED · PYTHON · LLM · GEODATA

tripforge

Rough trip notes in. A day-by-day itinerary, an interactive map and a costed PDF out.

notes.md → "vietnam, 10 days, me + 1, 1.5L each, old quarter street food is a MUST, ha long cruise…"
  1. Extract. Local LLM turns notes into a schema-checked brief, cached by notes hash.
  2. Geocode. OpenStreetMap lookups at 1 req/s, hits and misses cached.
  3. Plan. Days per city, neighbours grouped, must-sees guaranteed.
  4. Cost & check. Every rupee has a basis; errors stop the run.
  5. Render. Dark PDF, Leaflet map, JSON, DuckDB run history.
